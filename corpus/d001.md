# Home Loan Agreement - Clause 9.1 (Prepayment, floating rate)

Clause 9.1. No prepayment charge applies to home loans sanctioned on a floating rate of interest to individual borrowers. The borrower may prepay the whole or any part of the outstanding principal at any time without penalty, irrespective of the source of funds. This clause reflects the regulator's directive on floating-rate retail loans and overrides any charge described elsewhere in this agreement.
