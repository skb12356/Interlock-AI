# Home Loan - Documents required

Applicants must submit: 1. Identity and address proof (Aadhaar, passport or voter ID). 2. PAN card. 3. Income proof, being the last three salary slips and Form 16 for salaried applicants, or two years of audited financials for self-employed applicants. 4. Six months of bank statements. 5. Property title documents and the approved building plan.
